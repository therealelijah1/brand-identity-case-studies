# Logo evaluation criteria

Score each direction 1-5. Anything under 3 on legibility or scalability is out.

| Criterion | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Legibility | Can you read the wordmark at 16px and from across a room? |
| Scalability | Does it hold up as a favicon and on a billboard? |
| Distinctiveness | Would it be confused with a competitor? |
| Relevance | Does it express at least one of the chosen brand attributes? |
| Versatility | Does it work in one color, reversed, and on photos? |
| Longevity | Does it rely on a trend that will date in two years? |
