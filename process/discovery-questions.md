# Brand discovery questions

## Business
1. In one sentence, what do you do and for whom?
2. Who are your three closest competitors? What do they get right?
3. What do customers say when they recommend you?

## Personality
4. Pick three words your brand should feel like. Pick three it must never feel like.
5. If your brand were a person at a party, how would they behave?

## Practical
6. Where will the logo appear most: app icon, signage, social, packaging?
7. Are there colors or symbols you must keep or must avoid?
8. Who signs off on the final design?
